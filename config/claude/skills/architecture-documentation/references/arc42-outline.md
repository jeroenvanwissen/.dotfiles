# arc42 outline (12 chapters)

1. **Introduction and Goals:** business/system goals, stakeholders/roles, critical quality goals.
2. **Architecture Constraints:** technical, organizational, regulatory and operational constraints.
3. **Context and Scope:** system boundary and external interfaces/actors.
4. **Solution Strategy:** high-level choices and why they serve quality goals.
5. **Building Block View:** decomposition and responsibilities at useful levels.
6. **Runtime View:** relevant workflows, requests, events, exceptional cases.
7. **Deployment View:** infrastructure, environments, boundaries, scaling and operations.
8. **Crosscutting Concepts:** security, observability, data, resilience, API versioning, etc.
9. **Architecture Decisions:** index or summaries linked to ADRs; avoid copying full ADRs.
10. **Quality Requirements:** measurable quality scenarios and tradeoffs.
11. **Risks and Technical Debt:** documented risks, known debt, mitigations.
12. **Glossary:** system terms and meanings.

Use this as coverage guidance, not a demand for lengthy prose. Write "Not yet documented" where something important is truly unknown. Keep diagrams small, legible and source-controlled (e.g., Mermaid), and never invent infrastructure from framework conventions.
